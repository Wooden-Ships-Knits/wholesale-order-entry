**To:** Sales Representatives
**From:** PPIC
**Subject:** Introducing the Digital Order Form

Dear Team,

We are pleased to announce a new tool that our AI team has developed over the course of this summer: the **Digital Order Form**.

The Digital Order Form is a modern replacement for our paper order process, designed to make writing and submitting orders simpler and more reliable.

All F26 and S27 products have been loaded into the system. Entering a few letters of a style name will automatically populate the corresponding code, description, and available colors.

*(screenshot here: entering a style name — the code populates automatically and a dropdown displays the colors available for that style)*

The form is also connected to our customer list. Selecting an existing customer will fill in their details automatically.

All calculations are performed instantly as quantities are entered, eliminating the need for manual totals.

The form has been optimized for use on mobile phones and tablets, allowing orders to be written from anywhere without a laptop.

Altogether, we expect this tool to make order writing faster, reduce errors in codes, colors, and quantities, and speed up order submission after shows — allowing us to secure yarn sooner.

Please note that, effective **July 31**, hand-written orders can no longer be accepted for F26, S27, or any subsequent season.

You will have the opportunity to try the form firsthand in the coming days. We encourage you to explore it and share any feedback.

Kind regards,
PPIC
